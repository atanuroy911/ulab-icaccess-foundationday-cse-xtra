Keynote Session III

Keynote Session III Sat, 3 Oct, 02:30 PM – 03:30 PM · Online
Dr Rong Su
“Towards a Car-lite Society – Challenges and Opportunities”
Session Chair
Dr. Jia Uddin
Join online
https://bdren.zoom.us/j/8428351030?pwd=7kfJZ1PoRiAzd0aKSn3l28Yuta2D1P.1&omn=92475321723
Abstract

Private vehicles are well known to be one key contribution factor for traffic network inefficiency such as traffic jams, waste gas emissions and excessive traffic space requirements (due to parking needs). However, the average utilization rate of private cars is extremely low. How to reduce the number of private cars and their daily usage is one open question for the academia and industry. A car-lite society relies less on private cars for transport, emphasizing sustainable alternatives like walking, cycling, and public transport to reduce car dependency. One key challenge is how to convince people that their travel needs and quality of mobility will not degrade, even after they give up their private cars. One conjecture is that a person might be willing to consider this option only when he/she is certain that, whenever a travel-need arises, there will always be a suitable car available within a short period of time acceptable by the person. That is, the technologies must ensure a guaranteed maximum waiting time in any circumstances. In this talk, we will discuss how different mobility-related technologies might contribute to a holistic system solution to achieve a car-lite society, and what possible challenges are.

About the Speaker

Professor, School of Electrical & Electronic Engineering, Nanyang Technological University, Singapore
