Welcome

Computer Science & Engineering at ULAB

The Department of Computer Science and Engineering prepares graduates for a fast-changing digital world. Grounded in the liberal arts tradition, we combine rigorous technical training with critical thinking, communication and ethics. Our students and faculty are active in artificial intelligence, networks, cybersecurity, software engineering and embedded systems, in close collaboration with industry.
