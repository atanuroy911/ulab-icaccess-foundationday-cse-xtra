Welcome

Engineers with a liberal arts mind.

Our department prepares graduates for a fast-changing digital world. We pair rigorous technical training with critical thinking, communication and ethics, and our students work alongside faculty on research and industry projects from their first year.

  • Project-based curriculum with capstone and industry mentors
  • Active research in AI, networks, security and embedded systems
  • Internships, hackathons and a strong alumni network
